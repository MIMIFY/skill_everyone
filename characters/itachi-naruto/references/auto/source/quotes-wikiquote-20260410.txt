# Itachi Uchiha Quotes — Wikiquote

## Direct Quotes by Itachi

1. [Recurring gesture + line to young Sasuke]
   *Pokes Sasuke's forehead*
   "Sorry Sasuke, maybe next time."

2. [To young Sasuke, immediately after the clan massacre — constructing hatred]
   "You're not even worth killing. Foolish little brother, if you wish to kill me one day,
   foster your hatred and despise me, surviving in such an unsightly manner as this."

3. [To Sasuke — during Akatsuki arc]
   "You're still too weak. You don't have enough hate, and you know something? You never will."

4. [To Kakashi — demonstrating genjutsu]
   "I wonder how much of this someone without the Kekkai Genkai can take?"

5. [Implied / recurring — final battle]
   "Sorry Sasuke... this is the last time." [戦闘後、佐助の額を突いた後]

## Quotes About Itachi / Exchanges
