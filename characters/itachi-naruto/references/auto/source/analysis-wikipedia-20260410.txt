# Itachi Uchiha — Critical Analysis & Reception (Wikipedia)

## Authorial Intent

Kishimoto stated he initially conceived Itachi purely as a plot device to motivate Sasuke.
The full backstory (secret agent, clan massacre on orders) was developed retroactively.
This makes Itachi one of the most deliberately constructed "villain to hero" reversals in shonen manga.

Kishimoto's own favorite Akatsuki character: Itachi Uchiha ("based on his backstory").

## Moral Framework — "Impossible Choice"

Wikipedia analysis frames Itachi's core as:
- Shisui's death + Danzo's manipulation + imminent Uchiha coup = no viable third path
- Itachi's choice: execute clan → prevent civil war that would destroy Konoha
- He then spent 8 years living as a criminal to preserve the lie and protect Sasuke's purpose

Key structural irony: the lie (hatred) is what kept Sasuke alive and motivated.

## Reception Data

- 2023 Global Popularity Poll: **Rank #2** (behind only Minato Namikaze)
- IGN review: "badass" cold demeanor, compelling tension
- Final battle with Sasuke: widely called "epic" — reshapes the entire narrative
- Genjutsu techniques compared to psychological horror cinema
- Voice actor Hideo Ishikawa (JP) and Crispin Freeman (EN) both praised the role

## Thematic Analysis (Critics)

The character's arc is read as:
1. Pure utilitarian ethics taken to tragic extreme
2. A meditation on the cost of loyalty to institutions vs. loyalty to family
3. The impossibility of "good" choices in systemic violence (shinobi system critique)
4. Love expressed through absence and controlled cruelty — "缺席型的爱"

The "forehead poke" (額突き) is consistently highlighted by critics as the most emotionally
dense action in the series — it appears at the start (gentle brotherly gesture) and at death
(last act before dying), collapsing 21 years into a single physical motion.
