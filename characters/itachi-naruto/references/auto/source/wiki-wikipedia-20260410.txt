# Itachi Uchiha — Wikipedia

## Creation and Conception

Masashi Kishimoto initially had limited plans for Itachi's backstory, wanting only to give Sasuke motivation to kill him. However, Kishimoto later developed the concept of "Itachi as Konoha's secret agent who killed his clan under their orders." The character was originally conceived as leader of a 70-member assassination squad but was ultimately reimagined as an Akatsuki member. Kishimoto identified Itachi as his favorite among all Akatsuki characters based on his backstory.

The voice actors—Hideo Ishikawa (Japanese) and Crispin Freeman (English)—expressed satisfaction with portraying the character, with Ishikawa and Sasuke's voice actor eventually recognizing Itachi's role as a protective older brother.

## Role in the Story

Part I: Itachi debuts after the Third Hokage's death, using a Konoha infiltration as cover to monitor Sasuke's wellbeing. He reveals Akatsuki's goal to capture jinchūriki (tailed beast hosts).

Part II: Itachi eventually confronts Sasuke in their final battle. Before dying from illness, he plants protective measures including the Amaterasu technique and a crow containing Shisui's Sharingan.

Fourth Great Ninja War: Kabuto resurrects Itachi using forbidden jutsu. After regaining free will, Itachi seals Nagato and traps Kabuto using Izanami. He then shares his memories with Sasuke, revealing the truth about the clan massacre and expressing unconditional love for his brother.

## Character Analysis: The Moral Complexity

Itachi's background reveals nuance: he slaughtered the Uchiha clan not out of malice but to prevent a coup d'état and protect Konoha. His friend Shisui's suicide and Danzo's manipulation forced this impossible choice. The series gradually transforms audience perception from seeing him as villain to understanding him as tragic figure bound by duty.

## Reception

Critics widely praised Itachi's character development and fights. He ranked second in the 2023 global popularity poll, behind only Minato Namikaze. IGN called him "badass" and noted his cold demeanor creates compelling tension. Reviewers highlighted his final battle with Sasuke as "epic" and praised how his revelations reshape the narrative's trajectory. The character's visual techniques—particularly his genjutsu—drew comparisons to psychological horror cinema.
